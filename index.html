01 — About

From research papers to production agents

I'm a Computer Science graduate from VR Siddhartha Engineering College. I started out publishing research on Software-Defined Networking, then spent two years at Accenture building AI for industrial settings. That included multi-agent inspection workflows, reinforcement learning for robot arms, and real-time drone vision on NVIDIA DeepStream.

Today at GYTworkz I build backend systems for agentic AI products. I design LangGraph agents, text-to-SQL copilots, speech pipelines and organisation-wide knowledge systems that turn meetings, chats and emails into insights and action items.

I care about systems that are observable, reliable and useful, not just impressive in a demo.
